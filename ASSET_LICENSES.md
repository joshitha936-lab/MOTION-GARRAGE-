# MOTION GARAGE — Authentic Asset & Licensing Registry

Every vehicle 3D model, video clip, and photographic asset utilized in MOTION GARAGE is a genuine, licensed, real-world automotive asset sourced from verified archives (Sketchfab CC-BY, Three.js official repositories, PlayCanvas Web Components open-source repository, and Wikimedia Commons). No procedural, synthetic, or AI-generated vehicle assets are used.
